Kim Ji-Yeon

I am a Ph.D. student at POSTECH, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh.

My recent research focuses on reconstructing and generating 3D/4D human–object interactions. In particular, I am interested in how people interact with objects and each other, with the goal of building digital twins that simulate real-world interactions for content creation and robot learning.
